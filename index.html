How to use Go With the Flow

Debate flowing guide, speeches & keyboard shortcuts

Getting Started

Flowing is how debaters track arguments across speeches. It shows which points were answered and which were dropped.

Start a round: Click New round, type your debate topic or debater names, and begin flowing.
100% Offline: Everything auto-saves in your browser instantly. No account, sign-in, or internet connection needed.
Speeches & Flow Columns

Speeches advance horizontally from left to right in alternating constructive and rebuttal rounds:

1AC → 1NC → 2AC → 2NC/1NR → 1AR → 2NR → 2AR
Affirmative (Purple) Starts round in 1AC, defends contentions
Negative (Pink) Counters in 1NC, runs off-case arguments
Speed Flowing & Keyboard Shortcuts

Flow fast during rapid speeches without touching the mouse:

New point underneath Enter
Reply in next speech column → or Shift+Enter
Move between argument cards ↑ ↓ ← →
Unfocus or close modal Esc
Features & Useful Tools
Multiple Flow Sheets:

Click + aff or + neg in the left sidebar to add extra flow sheets for advantages or off-case positions.

Floating Debate Timer:

Click Timer in the top bar. Drag it anywhere across the screen—it stays visible and running while you flow.

Evidence Citations:

Click ev on any card to attach evidence excerpts, author credentials, and citations directly to your argument.

Backup & Export:

Click ··· in the top right to download a JSON backup file or generate a clean printable PDF flow sheet.
